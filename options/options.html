Price Watch

Settings

Check Interval

How often Price Watch reloads each product page in the background to fetch the latest price.

Notify Me When

A browser notification is sent when the current price drops below your target price. You can set a custom target per product in the popup.

💡 By default the target equals the price at the time you added the product. Lower the target in the popup if you want to wait for a bigger discount.

Data

All data is stored locally in your browser — nothing is sent to any server.
